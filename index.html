Common Homepage Problems We See Every Day

  • Missing LocalBusiness schema that helps Google Maps recommend your business
  • Phone numbers that search engines cannot easily read
  • Missing or weak meta descriptions in search results
  • No clear service area signals for local searches
  • Hidden indexing problems that block search visibility

Local Search Is About Trust, Not Traffic

When people search for a local service, they usually want to call a business or visit a location immediately. Search engines will not recommend a business unless the website clearly proves it is legitimate, safe, and relevant to the local area.

Missing technical signals or unclear contact information can quietly reduce how often your business appears in search results.
